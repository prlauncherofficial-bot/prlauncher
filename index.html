Features

First release, honest feature set.

prlauncher doesn't ship with big promises — just four things it tries to do well, right now.

Fabric & Forge support

Works directly with both of the most common mod loaders.

Simple settings menu

No maze of submenus — the setting you need is always one click away.

Customizable

Shape the interface to fit your own taste.

ely.by support

Sign in with your ely.by account, alongside your Microsoft account.

Install

Four steps to get playing.

01

Download

Grab the build for your OS from the Releases page on GitHub.

02

Run it

No installer needed — open the executable and the launcher starts.

03

Sign in

Log in with your Microsoft account or your ely.by account.

04

Create your first instance

Pick a version, add a mod loader if you want one, hit Play.

Ready to go?

prlauncher is free, and this is its first release. Fabric and Forge support, kept simple.
